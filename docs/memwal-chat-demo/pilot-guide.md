## Who to invite

Solo builders and small product teams (one to five people) who are deciding what to build next and keep re-explaining their strategy, constraints, research and past decisions to an assistant.

## Session plan (about 20 minutes, two sessions at least a day apart)

1. **Consent.** Explain what is stored where (facts on Walrus Mainnet, which is public and permanent; nothing is saved without their click), get the permission checkbox below, and give them their own workspace login. Each participant needs their own Walrus Memory account or namespace.
2. **Session 1.** Ask them to describe their product, audience, team and current priority, then to ask for advice on one decision. They review the suggested memories and save only what they agree with.
3. **Session 2 (another day, new conversation).** Ask a follow-up decision question without re-explaining. Note whether the assistant used the right facts, missed any, or recalled something irrelevant.
4. **Correction.** Ask them to state one change (for example, a new team size). Check whether the next answer prefers the newer fact.
5. **Feedback.** Three questions: what they no longer had to repeat, what was wrong or irrelevant, and whether they would use it again.

## What to record (no personal data beyond a participant code)

- Participant code (P1, P2, …), date, model used.
- Facts saved in session 1 (count) and whether they were edited before saving.
- Session 2: facts recalled, facts used correctly, facts missing, irrelevant recalls.
- Context the participant still had to repeat in session 2 (count of facts).
- Corrections made and whether the newer fact won.
- Quotes, only with permission.
